PhomemoPaint help

Draw in black and white and print on Phomemo thermal printers, no drivers needed.

Getting started

  1. Turn the printer on. There is no need to pair it in Windows: PhomemoPaint looks for it over Bluetooth when it starts and selects it. It shows up in the Printer panel as “T02 — Bluetooth …”.
  2. Draw. Paint with the brush , paste an image with Ctrl+V or drop an image file on the window.
  3. Set the length of the paper by dragging the blue line at the end of the canvas.
  4. Print with  or Ctrl+P. Only up to the last line with something drawn is printed, to save paper.
Press F1 with the mouse over any part of the window (canvas, tools, printer…) and this help opens right at that section.

Window layout

  1. Menus: File, Edit, View, Printer and Help.
  2. Rulers in millimetres, along the top and left.
  3. Paper: the canvas, with what you have drawn.
  4. Selected object, with its handles: an image or a text. A drawing can only be moved and has no handles.
  5. Orange line: how far would be printed.
  6. Blue line: the end of the paper. Drag it to change the length, or type it in the box next to it.
  7. Grey margin: it is not paper and is not printed.
  8. Tools: brush, images, text, cursor, colours and print.
  9. Properties of the selection.
  10. Printer: which one is used, its model and its details.
  11. Zoom.
  12. Status bar: mouse position in dots and millimetres, how much would be printed, yellow warnings and messages from the printer.

Properties

Shows the options of whatever is selected. With an image selected you can choose how it is turned into black and white (Dither or Threshold). With a text, its content and format (Text). With nothing selected, the panel says so.

 Printer

Choosing the printer

  • The first list holds the Bluetooth printers found and the COM ports. Bluetooth printers are searched for a few seconds when the window opens;  searches again. Printers seen before are remembered.
  • There is no need to pair the printer in Windows (it is Bluetooth LE and Windows does not list it).
  • The second list is the model. It is usually picked for you (from the Bluetooth name, the USB identifier or the serial number), but you can change it.
  • Label models (M110 family) also show the label width in mm.
The T02 only prints over Bluetooth. Over USB it answers queries but the paper comes out blank. If the same printer is seen over USB and Bluetooth, PhomemoPaint picks Bluetooth.

Printer information

When the printer is selected it is asked for its serial number, firmware, battery, paper and cover. The battery is read again every 5 minutes; the refresh button on that panel asks right away. Searching never sends anything to any device: only the printer you choose is queried.

Models that cannot print yet

Some A4 models are listed as “needs compression, cannot print”: you can pick them to draw at their width, but the Print button is disabled.

 Zoom

 zooms out,  zooms in (×1 to ×8) and  fits the canvas to the window width. Also with Ctrl+mouse wheel, Ctrl++, Ctrl+− and Ctrl+0. Zoom does not change what is printed.

The canvas

  • The width is the printer head width (the T02 prints 384 dots = 48 mm) and cannot be changed: switching printers crops or adds white on the right, it never scales.
  • The length is changed by dragging the blue line at the end of the canvas, by its handle or by the line itself. It snaps to whole millimetres; with Shift, dot by dot.
  • While dragging, what would be added shows as faint paper and what would be cut off, in red.
  • Next to the handle there is a box with the length in dots and its size in mm: you can type it too.
  • Below the canvas there is one screen of grey margin (not paper) so you can always drag the line down.
  • The dashed orange line marks how far would be printed: the last row with something drawn.
If you shorten the canvas and part of the drawing is lost, nothing is asked: a yellow warning shows at the bottom and Ctrl+Z undoes it.

 Brush

  • Left button: the chosen colour ( or ). Right button: the other one, to erase without switching colours.
  • The blue circle under the mouse shows the brush size.
  • What you paint forms a single object, a drawing, which keeps growing while you stay on the brush. It is closed by changing tools or with Esc; the next stroke starts another one. Enter fixes it to the paper.
  • A drawing can only be moved, with the  cursor: it cannot be scaled or rotated.
  • Ctrl+Z undoes the last stroke.
  • The brush never moves objects: it paints on top of them.

 Images

Adding

  •  or Ctrl+I to open a file, or drop the file on the window.
  • Ctrl+V pastes from the clipboard. Images copied from a web browser (“Copy image”) keep their transparency.
  • They are placed at the top of the visible area and, if wider than the paper, shrunk to its width. Adding an image switches to the  cursor so you can place it.

Whatever sticks out of the paper is shown faded and is not printed; a yellow warning reminds you. This lets you move an image half out and bring it back later.

 Text

  1. Choose the  tool (Ctrl+T) and click where the text should start.
  2. Type in the Properties box: the sample text is already selected and Enter adds a line.
  3. Click elsewhere to finish. The next click places another text.
  4. To change its box, drag the handles while the text is selected.
OptionWhat it does

The text sits inside a box. Lines break at its width and the alignments refer to it. When placed, the box reaches the edge of the paper. The handles change the box, with no fixed proportions and without touching the font size, and it can never be shorter than the text.

It is moved, rotated and fixed like an image. It stays editable until you fix it; click it to change it again. A text left empty disappears. Esc goes back from the editing box to the canvas.

Black and white

The printer only burns black dots. Colour and grey images are converted like this (in Properties, with the image selected):

  • Dither (default): spreads black dots to simulate greys. Best for photos.
  • Threshold: dark becomes black, light becomes white. Best for logos and line art.

Transparency is dithered too: a transparency gradient becomes dots further and further apart that let what is underneath show through. With Threshold, anything under 50% opacity is transparent.

 Printing

  • The full width is printed and, lengthwise, from the top down to the last line with something drawn (the orange line). Blank space left at the top is kept.
  • Before sending, the printer is checked for paper and a closed cover: if not, you are warned and nothing is sent (the printer would accept the job and say nothing).
  • While printing, the button becomes  Cancel and a progress bar shows at the bottom.
  • When done, the status bar tells how many lines were printed and what the printer answered. “Not confirmed” means the printer did not report the end (it sometimes doesn't); it usually prints anyway.

Warnings

PhomemoPaint does not interrupt you with dialogs about cropping. Warnings show in yellow on the status bar:

  • The drawing was cut when shortening the canvas or switching to a narrower printer (Ctrl+Z undoes it).
  • An image sticks out of the paper: that part will not be printed.
  • The chosen model needs compression and cannot print yet.

Calibration pattern

“Printer → Calibration pattern” replaces the drawing with a frame with marks every millimetre and a cross in the middle. Print it and measure it with a ruler: on the T02 the frame is 48 mm wide and, with the default length (400 dots), 50 mm tall.

Signal diagnostics

“Printer → Signal diagnostics” sends single commands to the printer and shows the answer in hex, as text and explained. It is a tool for the curious and for trying new models.

  • One command per line, in hex (1F 11 08). Anything after # is a comment.
  • It comes with the known queries: battery, firmware, serial number, auto power-off, paper, chip and capabilities.
Use it only with queries: an unknown command may change the printer settings. 1F 11 14, which starts a firmware update, is blocked.

Troubleshooting

The paper comes out blank

If the printer is connected over USB, switch to Bluetooth: the T02 does not print over USB. If it already is Bluetooth, check the roll (thermal paper only marks on one side).

The printer does not show up

  • Check it is on and press  to search again.
  • If it is connected to another device (your phone, for example) it does not advertise itself: disconnect it there.
  • After using it, Windows keeps the connection for a while and the printer stops advertising; if it was already in the list, it can still be used.

“Out of paper” or “cover is open”

Load a roll or close the cover and print again. With the cover open the printer also reports no paper.

“Sent, not confirmed”

The printer did not report the end. It usually prints anyway; if nothing came out, check the paper and the connection.

Wrong model

Pick it by hand in the model list. It will be remembered for that printer.
